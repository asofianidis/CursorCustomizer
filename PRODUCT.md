# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SpiritVale players who lose track of the game cursor during combat, over bright effects, or against terrain with similar contrast. Players need an immediate visual aid that does not alter clicking behavior.

## Product Purpose

Keep the cursor glanceable in active play through a configurable, client-side marker. Success means players can choose a clearly visible style in MCM, locate it instantly with a shortcut, and trust that the mod never changes the cursor hotspot or intercepts input.

## Brand Personality

Game-native, restrained, dependable. Visibility comes from contrast, outline, and scale instead of decorative effects.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the native cursor and exact click target.
2. Default to a familiar halo with strong light-and-dark contrast.
3. Apply bounded MCM changes immediately.
4. Keep the overlay non-interactive and suppress its shortcut during text entry.
5. Remove the overlay cleanly when the local character leaves the world.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide multiple shapes in addition to color, a broad but safe size range, high-contrast outlines, adjustable opacity, and a dedicated find-cursor pulse. Do not rely on motion or hue alone for everyday visibility.
